# Cloudy

From the team's character sheet. Lines in quotes are her actual words. Back to [all characters](../MENU_AND_CHARACTERS.md#characters).

## Basics
- **Animal and colours:** fox. Cream, white, teal.
- **Age vibe:** young adult, about 18, with a soft, kid-like vibe.
- **In town:** the local baker, with a passion for warm pastries.
- **Comes in with:** usually alone, sometimes with Vex if he's in the mood.
- **Concept art hints:** holding a warm mug of a hot drink with marshmallows.

## Personality
- **Three words:** cozy, gentle, shy.
- **Wants right now:** a warm drink, a quiet place, and some help with baking.
- **Worries about:** she lacks the skill for more complex or time-consuming pastries. She takes inspiration from Vex's high standards for everything.
- **Habit players will notice:** fidgets with the bell around her neck.
- **Clashes with:** nobody. Very rarely, and only for a short time, with someone who's incredibly annoying.

## Voice
- **How she talks:** soft voice, hesitant.
- **Gibberish voice:** high and bouncy.
- **Catchphrase:** "How cozy!" She says please a lot and thanks everyone.
- **Hello:** "Hello! May I please know if this seat is taken?"
- **Goodbye:** "Thank you so much for the service. Have a wonderful day."

## Drinks
**Her usual: Cozy Cup.** Like a hot chocolate with a splash of bourbon: sweet, creamy caramel in a mug, never iced.
- **Recipe:** Bourbon, then Dark Caramel (shake), then Syrup (shake). 3 units.
- **Taste:** Bitter 0, Sweet +4, Sour −1.
- **Served:** mixed, in a Mug, not cold, with Mint (her teal).
- **Loves:** warm mugs and whipped cream: the Mug.
- **Hates:** sour lemon juice (Lime Juice, Sour Mix, the Lemon garnish) and Ice.
- **Her own words for a drink:** "Cozy cup." "Warm milk for the soul." "Sweet as chocolate, oh wait it already has chocolate! My bad!"
- **Orders:**
  - Easy: "Can I please have a warm mug with sweet, sweet caramel and a little bourbon? No ice, please!"
  - A feeling: "May I please have something to make me feel like I'm in a warm cabin, cuddled in a warm blanket?"
  - A picture: "I'd like it to be fluffy and sweet like a chocolate river, please!"

On the original sheet her usual was milk + chocolate syrup + cream, in a warm mug. The game can't make a drink warm yet, only Cold, so "warm" means no ice for now. All four drinks are on [the characters page](../MENU_AND_CHARACTERS.md#their-drinks), and the rules tests make each one.

## Story
1. Cloudy wants to bake something special for [Vex](Vex.md)'s showcase, but she's too shy to give it to him herself.
2. She worries that baking doesn't fit the event, and that people, Vex included, will find it too childish.
3. She asks whether she should go ahead with the idea, and whether to give it in person or leave it somewhere anonymously.

**Quest.** "Should I hand Vex my gift directly, or leave it hidden as a secret?"

The quest is answered with the next drink you serve: its highest taste picks the answer. If that taste is neither answer, or the two answers tie, it's the third ending. Strong on the original sheet means Bitter, the game's "kick".

| Answer | She says | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Bitter (strong) | "I'll be direct with him. Make me something strong! I'll definitely need it." | She hands Vex the gift. He's flustered, praises her baking, and they talk a lot more after the event and become closer friends. Vex becomes kinder, and Cloudy more straightforward. |
| Sweet | "I don't want to make him feel awkward or anything, so just leave it as a sweet surprise." | She leaves the gift anonymously. Vex finds it and smiles softly. After the event he asks if she made it, she says yes, and they talk more about it. Vex becomes kinder, and Cloudy stays about the same. |
| Anything else | | She sells the pastry at her shop instead, and ends up a little quieter. |
