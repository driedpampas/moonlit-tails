# Sabre

From the team's character sheet. Lines in quotes are her actual words. Back to [all characters](../MENU_AND_CHARACTERS.md#characters).

## Basics
- **Animal and colours:** wolf. Grey, hot pink, black.
- **Age vibe:** rebel young adult, about 22.
- **In town:** underground rock musician, in a band with her friends.
- **Comes in with:** Razor and Sniper, her bandmates.
- **Concept art hints:** sharp, edgy energy, punk look.

## Personality
- **Three words:** edgy, fierce, passionate.
- **Wants right now:** the band's first successful rock concert in town.
- **Worries about:** the mixed reviews of their latest album.
- **Habit players will notice:** daydreams about playing the guitar, and taps her foot as if on a drum pedal.
- **Clashes with:** people who find rock music annoying.

## Voice
- **How she talks:** short, deep, very few words.
- **Gibberish voice:** low, a little melancholic.
- **Catchphrases:** "Time for a change of scene." "Not bad, rockstar."
- **Hello:** "Hit me up with something good."
- **Goodbye:** "I'll catch you later, rockstar."

## Drinks
**Her usual: Neon Storm.** A spiked cold brew: whiskey and espresso on ice, with cola for the kick the ginger syrup gave.
- **Recipe:** Whiskey, then Espresso (shake), then Cola (shake). 3 units.
- **Taste:** Bitter +4, Sweet +1, Sour 0.
- **Served:** mixed, in a Mug, Cold, with a Cherry.
- **Loves:** strong kicks: a high Bitter.
- **Hates:** Grenadine (the closest thing to strawberry syrup) and the Lemon garnish.
- **Her own word for a drink:** "Dark, intense, deep enough to make you scream your lungs out."
- **Orders:**
  - Easy: "Hey, I want a dark, strong whiskey brew. Espresso, cola, on ice. In a mug."
  - A feeling: "What's up, give me to strike me to the cord. And make it cold."
  - A picture: "Sup, make me the drink that reminds me of a thunderstorm in a neon-lit city."

On the original sheet her usual was cold brew + ginger syrup + crushed ice, in a mug. All four drinks are on [the characters page](../MENU_AND_CHARACTERS.md#their-drinks), and the rules tests make each one.

## Story
1. Sabre complains that her friends are always arguing about something.
2. They started arguing around when they began working on the last album.
3. She asks whether she should give them a big talk to stop it, or let them sort out their own disagreements.

**Quest.** "Should I go talk to them? They always seem heated whenever they're both together."

The quest is answered with the next drink you serve: its highest taste picks the answer. If that taste is neither answer, or the two answers tie, it's the third ending. Strong on the original sheet means Bitter, the game's "kick".

| Answer | She says | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Bitter (strong) | "Yea, I got the perfect plan. But for this, I'll need only the strongest hit." | She writes an emotional song about how sad it makes her to see her friends fight. Razor and Sniper are touched, come to terms with how the albums should go from now on, and give each other more leeway. They finish the album and have a successful first concert. All three are more communicative from now on. |
| Sweet | "Meh, I guess they should fix it on their own." | She chills and doesn't do much else. Things in the band don't get better, and it eventually breaks up. All three come in separately from now on. |
| Anything else | | She has a serious talk with them, because she's had enough of the arguing. They become a little more understanding, but some arguments still happen. Sabre is more talkative from now on. |

## Never
