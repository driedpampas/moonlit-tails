# Ember

From the team's character sheet. Lines in quotes are her actual words. Back to [all characters](../MENU_AND_CHARACTERS.md#characters).

## Basics
- **Animal and colours:** fox. Warm brown, red, cream.
- **Age vibe:** young adult, about 21, with playful, kid-like energy. She can talk with souls.
- **In town:** keeps the soul shrine, and specialises in soul magic.
- **Comes in with:** anyone who's down to hang out. She's rarely alone.
- **Concept art hints:** floating spirits, warm crimson, a floral aura.

## Personality
- **Three words:** silly, proud, playful.
- **Wants right now:** to teach others how to talk with spirits, and to make her magic stronger.
- **Worries about:** her magic is sometimes unstable, and can cause chaos if nobody notices.
- **Habit players will notice:** keeps adjusting her pins so they stay perfectly in place.
- **Clashes with:** old heads and nerds who think magic is just science that isn't understood yet.

## Voice
- **How she talks:** silly lines, some of which get incredibly grand and formal.
- **Gibberish voice:** energetic, vibrant, soulful.
- **Catchphrases:** "Whisper me this." "Spirits are listening!"
- **Hello:** "Hayo, my name's Ember, and I'm the one and only spirit caster in town!"
- **Goodbye:** "Departing, my friends! May see you on the other side, hihi!"

## Drinks
**Her usual: Spirit Order.** Two layers in a stemmed glass: a red one like cranberry and strawberry, and a dark caramel one where the chocolate was.
- **Recipe:** two prepared drinks, layered. Bottom: Bourbon, then Grenadine (shake). Top: Bourbon, then Dark Caramel (shake). One pour of each.
- **Taste:** Bitter −1, Sweet +6, Sour 0.
- **Served:** layered, in a Coupe, with a Cherry. Not cold.
- **Loves:** tiny particles and vibrant colours: layered drinks and a garnish.
- **Hates:** plain water (Tonic Water is the closest) and anything with Bitter above 0.
- **Her own word for a drink:** "The order of all spirits together."
- **Orders:**
  - Easy: "Hayo, I'd like a stemmed glass with two layers, please! Bourbon and grenadine at the bottom, bourbon and dark caramel on top, and a cherry!"
  - A feeling: "I need something sweet enough to make any soul happy. Make it as vibrant as possible!"
  - A picture: "Summon everything to create the order of all spirits!"

On the original sheet her usual was cranberry juice + strawberry syrup + chocolate syrup, in a stemmed glass. All four drinks are on [the characters page](../MENU_AND_CHARACTERS.md#their-drinks), and the rules tests make each one.

## Story
1. Ember is trying to make a new spell that summons a magic carpet to travel at lightning speed.
2. She keeps failing, and wastes a lot of her energy on it.
3. Her only hope is to travel to the ancient mausoleum and learn from the spirit grandmaster how to cast better spells.

**Quest.** "You think he will be able to teach me? What if I'm just simply, not good enough to do this?"

| Answer | She says | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Sweet | "Yummy! Alright, I've decided I will go find him! Worst case, I simply get to meet new folks and maybe make a new friend!" | She goes to the mausoleum. The grandmaster has sadly passed away, but he left a book with all his spells. With it she casts spells she's never seen before, including the flying carpet. **Ember doesn't come back to the bar**: she's busy trying out new spells. |
