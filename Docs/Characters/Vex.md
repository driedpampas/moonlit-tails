# Vex

From the team's character sheet. Lines in quotes are his actual words. Back to [all characters](../MENU_AND_CHARACTERS.md#characters).

## Basics
- **Animal and colours:** cat. Navy blue and gold.
- **Age vibe:** young adult, about 20.
- **In town:** fashion critic.
- **Comes in with:** usually alone, sometimes with Cloudy.
- **Concept art hints:** a stemmed glass of bright orange drink with an orange slice.

## Personality
- **Three words:** proud, ambitious, confident.
- **Wants right now:** to become a world-famous fashion designer and critic.
- **Worries about:** people not taking him seriously and thinking he's an amateur. He wants more respect.
- **Habit players will notice:** keeps adjusting his metal necklace, and always tries to keep his coat clean.
- **Clashes with:** anyone who doesn't give him the respect he needs.

## Voice
- **How he talks:** very formal, usually long sentences with a dramatic pause in between.
- **Gibberish voice:** slightly bouncy.
- **Catchphrases:** "Outstanding." "Obviously." "Finally."
- **Hello:** "Greetings, (player name). Make it your best today."
- **Goodbye:** "Keep your standards high, (player name). Have a good one."

## Drinks
- **His usual:** lemon juice + orange juice + simple syrup, in a stemmed glass.
- **Loves:** stemmed glasses and gold hues.
- **Hates:** thick mugs, milk, cream.
- **His own words for a drink:** "The golden standard." "Stemmed glass for the stars." "Crisp gold, with a small kick to it."
- **Orders:**
  - Easy: "Give me a stemmed glass filled with your sharpest citrus blend."
  - A feeling: "Give me something to invigorate me on this mundane day."
  - A picture: "Bring on the drink to match my sense of fashion and my amber glow."

Some of these ingredients aren't in the game yet: see [Drinks the characters ask for](../MENU_AND_CHARACTERS.md#drinks-the-characters-ask-for).

## Gift
- **Favourite:** a golden bell. "Oh wow... I didn't expect anyone to give me a gift like this. Looks like you truly know how to please people, don't you?"
- **Disliked:** a bronze mini statue. "Oh that's... not fitting my standards, as you obviously missed that I'm all about the riches. Only gold and silver is what matters."

## Story
1. Vex brags about the upcoming fashion showcase, where he's the guest of honour.
2. He mentions that nobody has congratulated him on it yet.
3. He asks whether he should change paths and try something more approachable, something that would make his peers like him more.

**Quest.** "Should I keep this distinguished style for the showcase, or should I try experimenting with something more 'casual'?"

| Answer | He says | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Strong | "Make it as strong as my brilliance and mastery of the craft!" | The showcase goes ahead as normal, but only a few of the elite show up, and none of his peers. Vex is quieter from now on. |
| Sweet or Fizzy | "Hold on some of the kick this time. I want people to see something a little cozier." | He brings a more casual style. Some of the professionals leave, but his friends show up instead, and he makes a few new ones. Vex is more casual from now on. |
| Neither | | He's stuck on the dilemma and drops out as guest of honour. Vex is quieter from now on. |

## Never
- Drinks from a mug, or from anything that isn't a stemmed glass.
- Slouches in his chair.
- Associates with anyone who isn't among the best.
